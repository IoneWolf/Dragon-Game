# 2026-08-30 - Level Navigation And Spawns

## Completed

- Built a reusable `LevelExit` and `LevelSpawnPoint` workflow for travel between Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3.
- Added `LevelSpawnPoint.prefab`, which displays a cyan marker during gameplay and a green gizmo in the Scene view.
- Established directional spawn IDs:
  - `FromLeft` for arrivals from the level on the left.
  - `FromRight` for arrivals from the level on the right.
- Configured forward/reverse travel:
  - Level 1 right exit -> Level 2 `FromLeft`.
  - Level 2 left exit -> Level 1 `FromRight`.
  - Level 2 right exit -> Level 3 `FromLeft`.
  - Level 3 left exit -> Level 2 `FromRight`.
- Added Level 3 to Build Settings and all serialized `GameController.levelScenePaths` lists.
- Created `Persistent.unity` as the startup scene. It owns the `GameController`, looping music, and the sole active `AudioListener`.
- Changed transitions to asynchronous additive loading: the destination loads alongside the current content scene, then the former content scene unloads. Persistent and music remain loaded throughout.
- Preserved the traveling Player across scene changes, removed any duplicate Player instantiated by a destination scene, and placed the retained Player at the destination spawn point.
- Updated camera handoff behavior: snap to the arriving Player and set camera smoothing to zero for a scripted `0.05` seconds before restoring normal smoothing.
- Moved the player HUD ownership to `Persistent.unity`. Health and game-over UI now rebind to the retained player's `PlayerHealth` after the initial level loads and after every transition.
- Added an objective tracker for defeating the red square and talking to the yellow NPC. The tracker persists its completion state, but displays only during gameplay levels; `Tab` expands or minimizes it.
- Added `SpikeTrap.prefab`: an upward-pointing triangle trigger that deals one damage and, when the player survives, respawns them at their last safe grounded position.
- Added hold-to-crouch on left or right Ctrl. Crouching halves the player sprite height only and restores its initial height on release.
- Set hold-to-sprint on left or right Shift to exactly double player movement speed and restore base speed on release.

## Authoring Rules

- Use one `LevelSpawnPoint.prefab`; do not make separate left/right prefabs.
- Every possible entrance to a level needs a placed spawn-point instance with the matching `spawnId`.
- Every scene in the linear route must be listed in Build Settings and in Persistent's `GameController.levelScenePaths` in travel order.
- Each content scene has a scene-owned camera but no active `AudioListener`; Persistent owns the single listener.
