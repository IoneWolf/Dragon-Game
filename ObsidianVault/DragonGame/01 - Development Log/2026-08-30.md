## Authoring Rules

- Use one `LevelSpawnPoint.prefab`; do not make separate left/right prefabs.
- Every possible entrance to a level needs a placed spawn-point instance with the matching `spawnId`.
- Every scene in the linear route must be listed in Build Settings and in `GameController.levelScenePaths` in travel order.
- Each level should have one Main Camera and one AudioListener.
